Accessing This App on Your Phone

Because this web app runs right in your browser, you can easily open it on your smartphone while working at your bakery workbench!

Option 1: Quick Free Hosting (Recommended)

To have a permanent link you can bookmark on Safari or Chrome on your phone:

  1. Save this HTML code file as index.html on your computer.
  2. Upload it for free in seconds to Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages.
  3. Open the generated public link on your phone browser and save it to your phone's home screen!

Option 2: Add to Home Screen as an App

  • iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen, then select "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the three dots menu in the top right, then select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
